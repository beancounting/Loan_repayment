How It Works

This calculator estimates the maximum home price you can afford based on conventional lending guidelines.

Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratios

Lenders use two DTI ratios to determine how much you can borrow:

  • Front-End Ratio (28%): Your total housing costs (mortgage payment, taxes, insurance, HOA, PMI) should not exceed 28% of your gross monthly income.
  • Back-End Ratio (36%): Your total housing costs plus all other debts should not exceed 36% of your gross monthly income.

The calculator uses whichever ratio is more restrictive to determine your maximum affordable home price.

Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)

If your down payment is less than 20% of the home price, lenders typically require PMI. This calculator estimates PMI at 0.7% of the loan amount per year. PMI is included in the DTI calculation, which reduces your maximum affordable price.
