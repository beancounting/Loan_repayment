How It Works

Principal & Interest (P&I)

Each payment covers both interest on the remaining balance and a portion of the principal. The fixed payment is calculated using the amortization formula:

M = P × [r(1+r)n] / [(1+r)n − 1]

Where P = principal, r = per-period interest rate, n = total number of payments.

Interest Only

Each payment covers only the interest. The principal balance remains unchanged throughout the loan term, with the full principal due as a balloon payment at the end.

M = P × r

Payment Frequencies

  • Monthly: 12 payments per year
  • Bi-weekly: 26 payments per year (equivalent to roughly 13 monthly payments)
  • Weekly: 52 payments per year

Choosing bi-weekly or weekly payments effectively makes extra payments each year, reducing total interest and shortening the loan term for P&I loans.

Extra Payments

Any extra payment goes directly toward reducing the principal balance. This reduces the interest charged on future payments and can significantly shorten the loan term. Extra payments work with both P&I and Interest Only loans.
